Our research focuses on Trustworthy Visual Intelligence: understanding, authenticating, and protecting visual content against manipulation, misinformation, and misuse, while advancing the theoretical foundations of robust and secure AI-driven visual systems. We currently pursue three directions:

  • Multimedia Forensics. Developing techniques to detect and localize manipulated visual content, while protecting users from their harmful effects.
  • Model Security. Identifying and mitigating threats to vision systems, including adversarial attacks, data security risks, and privacy leakage.
  • Visual Analysis. Advancing computer vision and machine learning methods for reliable visual understanding and representation.
