I am an Associate Professor at Ocean University of China, where I lead the Visual Analysis & Security (VAS) Lab. My research focuses on Trustworthy Visual Intelligence, with particular interests in multimedia forensics, vision security, and machine learning. My work has been published in leading venues with over , including several works with more than citations each ( CVPR20, CVPRW19, ICASSP19, WIFS18). The work of WIFS18 was also featured on CCTV13’s "World Weekly" special on deepfakes [7:43]. I have been recognized on Stanford/Elsevier Top 2% Scientists List (since 2023) and received the ACM Qingdao Rising Star Award (2024). I currently serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security and Pattern Recognition. [中 CN]

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University at Albany, SUNY in 2020 advised by Siwei Lyu (IEEE/IAPR/AAIA Fellow), and was subsequently a Senior Research Scientist at the University at Buffalo, SUNY. I received M.S. degree in 2015 and B.S. degree in 2012 at Shandong University.
